Why Egypt

A thousand years of scholarship

Scholarship

Al-Azhar and a living tradition

Founded in 970 CE, Al-Azhar is one of the world's oldest universities. Cairo's scholarly tradition is continuous and living — teachers here trace their isnads back through generations of ʿulamāʾ. This is not a museum exhibit of Islamic learning; it is the real thing.

970CE — Year Al-Azhar was founded
01
Immersion

Arabic surrounds you in every direction

Every shop, street, conversation, and argument is in Arabic. The daily immersion accelerates progress in ways no classroom in a Western country can replicate. You will absorb more in six months of living in Nasr City than in two years of weekly lessons at home.

عربيEvery sign, every conversation
02
Affordability

Exceptional value against UK tuition costs

Institute fees from $50–200 per level. Shared accommodation from £70/month. Total living costs for a solo student from £400–600/month. For comparison, UK university tuition alone runs about £9,250 a year — a full year of study, housing and living costs in Cairo can come in below that figure.

03
Community

A global brotherhood and sisterhood

Students from France, Russia, Norway, Indonesia, the UK and USA study side by side in Nasr City. The bonds formed here last a lifetime. Experienced students guide newcomers, share housing leads, and warn of safety updates daily. The community is the infrastructure.
